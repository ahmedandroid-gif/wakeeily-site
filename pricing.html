Prices in Egyptian pounds, with nothing hidden.

Every plan is priced in pounds and billed in pounds, excluding VAT. There is no dollar line and no foreign AI subscription behind it: your documents are read on our own machine in Egypt.

The founding offer

The first 5 clients get 30 % off their plan for 12 months. The offer closes on 31 December 2026, or earlier, when the five places are taken. We start two new pilots a month, so the places go in order of signature.

How every client starts

In every plan

  • Nothing to install: a phone or a laptop and a browser.
  • Sign in with a code sent to your email, then your own password; no app to install.
  • Upload and ask, in Arabic or English, and get the quoted passage with every answer.
  • Your documents are processed on our own machine in Egypt, and no cloud model reads them.
  • Scanned pages are read too, and every figure taken from a scan is flagged for a person to check; scanned pages beyond your plan's share are quoted per batch.
  • Large uploads are queued and read in the background, so your live questions stay fast.
  • Prices exclude VAT; invoices are in Egyptian pounds.
