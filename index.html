Ask your documents. Get the answer — with the proof.

Wakeeily reads your contracts, invoices and correspondence, in Arabic and English, and answers your questions, quoting the exact passage each answer comes from.

What we do

  • Cited answers. Ask in Arabic or English: each answer names the passage and the version of the document it came from, and says "not in the documents" when the answer isn't there. On invented test documents in Arabic and English, 28 of 28 answerable questions were answered correctly.
  • Contract and invoice checks. A nine-point contract review with the quoted evidence for each point, and invoice figures checked against the source text; 40 of 40 invented test invoices came back with their totals, VAT and line amounts correct.
  • Routine paperwork, done for you. An AI agent turns your files into ready-to-use tables and registers, and your team reviews every result before using it.

Why it's private

Your documents are processed on our own private machine, and no cloud model ever reads them. This website uses no cookies and no tracking tools.
